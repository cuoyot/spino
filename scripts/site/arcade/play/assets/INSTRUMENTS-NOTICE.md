# Instrument samples

Piano, acoustic bass, fingered electric bass, nylon guitar, cello, violin and soprano saxophone samples from FluidR3 by Frank Wen, packaged for MIDI.js by gleitz.

Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
License: CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

G2–G5 MP3 data extracted without audio modification. Playback applies instrument-specific volume scaling and a release envelope; no sustain looping.
