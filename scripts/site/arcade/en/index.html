Version 0.9 · Coming to Google Play

Music becomes a game

Spino Arcade is a game born from Spino: Smart Piano Notebook. Tap the on-screen keyboard when each moving note reaches the timing frame.

  • Four starting difficulties and gentle early-game protection.
  • Timing bonuses, streaks and rechargeable gold hearts.
  • Treble/bass clefs and keyboard-position changes with visible pauses.
  • Familiar melodies, musical links and seven sampled instruments.
  • English, Spanish and Catalan. Portrait and landscape.

Offline play, without accounts, ads or purchases.
