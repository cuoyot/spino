# Acoustic grand piano samples

These 37 MP3 samples (G2–G5) are extracted without audio modifications from the acoustic grand piano in FluidR3_GM, packaged by gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts for MIDI.js.

- Original soundfont: FluidR3 by Frank Wen.
- Distribution and source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
- Source file: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes: selected G2–G5 samples and extracted the original MP3 data into individual files. Playback applies a volume envelope without a sustain loop.

Sharp pitches use enharmonic flat filenames (for example C♯4 uses Db4.mp3).
