A place for every kind of practice

Read at your own pace

Fresh sight-reading and technique exercises, one hand or both. Choose a tempo or practise without a clock. Rhythm and range follow your pianist level.

Warm up before you play

An optional, rotating set of Hanon exercises at the start of piano practice, with step-by-step hand-position illustrations.

Connect your piano

USB or Bluetooth MIDI checks supported note responses, including chords. An acoustic piano works too, with manual practice tracking. Configure pedals for page turns and follow supported MusicXML passages with MIDI.

Routines that fit your day

Create, reorder and time your practice blocks. Schedule a saved routine from the calendar and choose your tempo when you practise.

Your scores, close at hand

Keep pieces on your music stand, import PDF and MusicXML, and explore available catalogue editions. Annotate PDFs, add note-linked MusicXML fingering, save framing and print your scores. PDF import can suggest title, composer and printed tempo locally, including scans.

A quick musical challenge

One tap opens on-screen practice: no tempo, one hand and a random clef and keyboard position. Earn points for accuracy and speed, and beat your personal best.

Find your pulse

A dedicated free-practice workspace with audio, visual or combined metronome signals, tempo and volume faders, tap tempo and favourite tempos.

See your practice grow

Review saved sessions, piece histories, feedback and weekly goals. Beta 6.1 adds a 12-week time chart and detailed ranges with combined activity and piece filters. Edit completed practice when its times or blocks need correcting. Technique compares response accuracy after enough comparable practice blocks.

Record your progress · beta 6.1

Record audio or video linked to a repertoire piece. Start and stop from the floating score bar, review each take and browse Library → Multimedia. Files stay on this installation; media cloud copies and sharing are not available yet.

Make space for lessons and practice

Switch between week and month, schedule routines and add recurring lessons or events with an optional end date. Supported devices can remind you before planned practice and appointments.

Back up and connect your notebook

Export portable JSON backups with scores and annotations. Optional synchronization uses a notebook file you choose through your device’s provider. A QR or connection code helps connect another device; each device grants its own file access. Recordings remain local and are excluded from these backups and sync. Continue locally when a provider is unavailable.

Practise in your own way

Choose English, Spanish or Catalan; light, dark or device appearance; pianist level, clefs, hands and exercise settings. Use the on-screen keyboard without a piano, or retain your scores and history for practice offline.

Your notebook, your choice

No app account, ads or SPino-operated backend. The notebook and recordings stay on your device. Optional notebook backup and sync use a file chosen through your device’s provider, which may require its own account. Media recordings are not included in JSON backups or synchronization.

SPino 6.0.0-beta.3 (42)

Profile now offers Connect another device: show the connected notebook's QR, scan it with the new device's camera, or enter the connection code. The QR contains a notebook UUID and filename, never cloud credentials, local permission bookmarks, scores or user account tokens. It identifies a notebook; the new device still grants its own access by selecting the matching file in Google Drive/iCloud. Android and iOS native pickers check the identity before replacing the current destination. Automatic cloud synchronization keeps its existing JSON data format and merge/recovery behavior. Legacy shared files receive additive identity metadata with a compare-and-swap write; no record IDs or notebook schema versions change.

Manual exports offer compatible JSON format 1 and organized JSON format 2. The organized copy keeps notebook state, repertoire, scores, annotations and score views in separate sections in one portable file. Original asset and annotation IDs, score bytes, musical titles and user-entered text are retained. No external directory is required. Format 2 requires beta 3 or later; explicit acknowledgement explains that older apps cannot open that new copy. Format 1 remains the default. Convert a backup writes a separate output file in either direction, preserving the source and local notebook. Restoration recognizes both formats, previews annotation counts and conversion, and requires the existing replacement confirmation before writing. Unknown future formats are refused with an update message; no speculative migration or destructive in-place conversion is attempted.

SPino 6.0.0-beta.2 (41)

Owner-requested beta practice adjustments, 2026-10-12. Stable remains 5.2.3 (35).

  • A fixed header keeps Profile at the top left, the page title and library/calendar/quick-practice controls in the middle, and MIDI at the right. The page content scrolls independently. Header content has a 68 px minimum height in both orientations, in addition to device safe-area padding for notches and the home indicator. Existing tablet/landscape side navigation stays in place; phone bottom navigation has five destinations. Immersive generated practice and score readers cover/hide the app chrome. Light-mode MIDI uses a pale button and legible neutral/green strokes.
  • New practices start with the timer running. Recovered sessions remain paused so closed-app time is not silently counted. Warm-up explanations and explicit pauses release the screen-awake policy. Opening the generated reader preserves seconds already counted.
  • Done editing resumes PDF practice only after annotations and view changes have saved. A normal pause remains distinct from editing, permits finishing, and releases the native screen-awake lock. Save failures keep the practice paused.
  • Starting practice checks configured synchronization and offers a direct route to conflicts or connection management. A new incoming notebook is adopted before practice starts; the reloaded screen must be reviewed before starting again.
  • Configured, enabled cloud destinations automatically save changes across all screens, including practice. Scheduling is bounded rather than repeatedly postponed by practice checkpoints. Incoming changes are deferred while practice or editing is active; revisions, recovery snapshots and compare-and-swap writes remain in place. No cloud account or destination is connected without user selection.
  • Feedback includes editable practiced minutes. Original measured duration is retained in recordedElapsedMs; activity totals are adjusted consistently. IDs, notes, measurements and musical material are preserved.
  • Short MIDI press when disconnected refreshes input ports and attempts the most recently remembered Android BLE instrument; holding opens MIDI settings. Connected short presses open settings. Readers save edits and close safely before navigating. iOS refreshes Core MIDI ports and uses Apple's Bluetooth panel if inputs remain unavailable; silent reconnection of an unavailable BLE instrument is controlled by the system, not an Android address.
