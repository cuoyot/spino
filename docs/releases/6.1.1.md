# SPino 6.1.1 (86) — stable practice recording release

## Release notes

Practice recording in audio and video, with start/end trimming. Shared and downloaded videos receive a three-second opening card with the SPino logo, spinoapp.com, piece title and recording date, followed by the selected recording and a two-second closing card with the logo and website. Each segment fades to/from black over 300 ms. Cards follow the recording orientation. Audio-only recordings remain audio files. Export leaves the saved take available for further editing and avoids repeated cards.

Revised and expanded catalogue, improved Google Drive synchronization, and repertoire integration into in-person lessons. This release promotes the beta functionality to stable. A matching APK is provided for existing beta installations.

## Delivery

The production APK and AAB retain the existing production application identity and signing configuration. The beta-channel APK retains its existing identity and certificate. Google Play rollout is a separate action; website publication does not change the installed Play Store version. Export cancellation or failure leaves saved recordings and notebook data untouched.
