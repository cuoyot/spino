# SPino 6.1.1-beta.1 (77)

## Release notes

Practice seconds now repaint when they change, independently of periodic checkpoints. Unchanged seconds retain their text nodes, and the floating PDF/MusicXML clock uses fixed-width digits.

In PDF and MusicXML, tapping the clock while editing saves pending changes, closes the editing tools and leaves practice paused. Tap the clock again to resume. The separate Done editing action and Back retain their existing save-and-resume behavior. Failed saves leave editing paused for retry.
