# SPino 6.1.5 (103) — Drive synchronization and complete backups

## Release notes

History synchronizes before score downloads, with progress and retries. Simpler Drive connection and a recoverable local copy before opening another notebook without merging. Conversion requires confirmation that every device is updated. Keep the latest 7 complete ZIP backups, created by the principal device after changes. Automatic backups require the app to be open; ZIP limit 250 MB.

## Validation limits

Promotes 6.1.5-beta.3. Physical two-device Google Drive and cellular acceptance remain unverified. Existing notebook data, IDs, scores, annotations and recordings are preserved. Complete automatic backups require the app to be open and are limited to 250 MB.
