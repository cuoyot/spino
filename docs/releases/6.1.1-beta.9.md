# SPino 6.1.1-beta.9 (85) — recording trim and copy recovery

## Release notes

Trim saved audio and video at the beginning and end, preview the selected segment and replace the local recording. Titles, stable local record IDs and piece/session associations are preserved. Save failures retain the original bytes. Existing Drive backups remain under the additive backup policy; prior revisions are excluded from reimport on the editing device.

Recording copy recovery accepts valid quoted codec metadata, identifies per-recording problems and continues independent copies. Retry is available after correcting a problem. A practice open on another device is not the cause of the metadata-validation error.
