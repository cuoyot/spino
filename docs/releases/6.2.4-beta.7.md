# SPino 6.2.4-beta.7

Android versionCode 135. Website beta, 27 November 2026.

## Changes

Review performed activities, passages and the total recorded duration at the end of a practice. Tap a value to correct it. The suggested name uses the practice type and time of day. Notes and next steps open from their own tiles. The mood selector sits immediately above Save. Initial focus starts on the heading, with a visible focus indicator on Close when navigating by keyboard.
