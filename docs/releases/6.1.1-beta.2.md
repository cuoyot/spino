# SPino 6.1.1-beta.2 (78)

Android beta candidate with the owner-approved articulated Hanon model extended to all ten existing exercises. The corrected signed package, replacement emulator installation and complete native playback are verified; website publication follows the release commit.

## Release notes

Animated hand demonstrations now follow Hanon Nos. 1–10 through the complete ascending and descending cycle. One fixed keyboard highlights the left hand in blue and the right hand in orange. Released fingers gather, prepare and reopen independently while the playing finger keeps its contact. Play slowly, advance by note or inspect either hand separately. The warm-up card shows the matching starting position.
