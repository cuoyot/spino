# SPino 6.1.4-beta.3 (94) — Recording controls

## Release notes

Recording is integrated into the movable and minimizable score widget. Unified format selection, visible disconnected MIDI guidance, compact MIDI playback with play/pause and shared export icons.

## Validation

Synthetic browser checks cover capture, export, playback, score movement and collapse. Physical MIDI acceptance remains separate. Website beta delivery only; no Play Console submission.
