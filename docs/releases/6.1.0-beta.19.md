# SPino 6.1.0-beta.19 (72)

## Release notes

The camera starts with the full field of view at 1×. Recording requests Full HD where supported, with a higher video bitrate. Camera settings provide adjustable zoom and fixed image brightness, plus exposure lock on compatible cameras. The preview and saved video use the same framing and brightness.

Choose vertical 9:16 or horizontal 16:9 before recording, and rotate the image in 90° steps. A compact full-screen toolbar and collapsible settings leave more room for the preview. Title and date remain upright in the saved video.

After saving, the viewer stays open with Share. Saved audio and video also have a Share button in Multimedia and playback. Android opens its system sharing chooser with the original file. Supported browsers open their sharing sheet; other browsers download the file. Cancelling preserves the recording and notebook.
