# SPino 6.1.0-beta.15 (68)

## Release notes

Progress pairs a musical weekly streak with this week's audio/video recordings. Tap the rhythm card for a monthly practice calendar and streak details; tap recordings to open Multimedia at the current week. The existing weekly practice and goal rules are preserved.

Multimedia opens with a week. Tap the month/year heading to expand the month and tap again to return; arrows browse the displayed period and the recording list follows. Boundary weeks name both months and years when needed. Only recorded dates receive color: teal audio, coral video and combined mixed dates, with separate per-type count rows. There are no extra filter controls or explanatory paragraphs.

The visual History approved in the companion review is included: local score thumbnails, colored practice icons, prominent existing feedback and whole-card session opening. Today also uses hours/minutes for saved weekly time and its goal. Notebook records, PDFs, annotations and original recording identities remain unchanged by the presentation updates.

## Validation and delivery

Production package, focused browser/unit checks, native replacement installation and public delivery verification are recorded separately in the release manifests. Native review uses disposable storage without clearing the installed notebook. Stable Android 6.0.0/build53, Google Play 5.2.3/build35 and the iOS Simulator download remain unchanged.
