# SPino 6.2.4-beta.2

Android versionCode 130. Website beta authorized on 26 November 2026 after preparation of the first eight interface proposals and the owner's icon-only repetition and quiet-scrollbar refinements.

## Changes

Cleaner practice preparation with collapsed inactive warm-up settings, consistent practice modes and reachable form actions. Calendar rows show multiple activities and planning makes unavailable past weekdays clear. Improved empty-collection messages, contextual accessible action names, playable keyboard-key names and disconnected MIDI settings entry.

Event repetitions open from an icon beside Time. Horizontal practice-card scrollbars are hidden; browsing scrollbars are hidden on narrow/touch devices and softened on desktop. Touch, trackpad and keyboard scrolling remain available.
