# SPino 6.1.0-beta.7 (60)

## Release notes

Adds an isolated Android Google Drive transfer check in Profile. Connect your own Google account, upload a synthetic two-minute recording on one device, then find, download and play it on another device using the same account and test notebook code. Uploads retain resumable progress; downloaded bytes must match their SHA-256 checksum. Personal recordings, notebook synchronization, PDFs and annotations are not accessed by this check. Automatic multimedia synchronization and migration are not enabled. Google authorization remains in testing until the developer completes production setup; test accounts must be listed in Google Auth Platform.

Fixes recording playback progress jumping ahead by finalizing missing WebM duration for new and existing takes. Saved recording viewers now offer Delete as a red link and Close, with a pencil to edit the title and a checkmark to save without restarting playback. New takes retain Save/Discard.

## Validation and delivery

Protocol tests cover two synthetic devices, restart after interrupted and completed uploads, stable file IDs, account/notebook separation, rejected corrupt bytes and URL restrictions. Browser checks cover synthetic transfer, decoding, original recording capture and playback. Android compilation, replacement installation, notebook retention and foreground evidence are recorded separately. Actual Google authorization, cross-device discovery and physical playback are owner acceptance gates; simulated results do not establish them.

Stable Android 6.0.0/build53, beta6 and iOS Simulator beta9 artifacts are retained. This website beta does not publish to Google Play or TestFlight.
