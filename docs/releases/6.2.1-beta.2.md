# SPino 6.2.1-beta.2

Android versionCode 114. Website beta delivery based on 6.2.1-beta.1 (113); stable 6.2.0 (112) remains available.

## Changes

Other activity and routine controls are muted and disabled until the session is finished, saved or discarded. Navigation remains available. Existing flexible practice and synchronization improvements are retained.
