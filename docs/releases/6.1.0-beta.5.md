# SPino 6.1.0-beta.5 (58)

## Release notes

Black piano keys retain their dark material in light mode. The keyboard previously inherited the white background used for general controls. Idle keys, pressed black keys, red note guides and decorative partial keys now keep their own colours in both themes.

The same correction applies to the miniature scale keyboard. Key positions, MIDI mapping, range, zoom and practice results are preserved. Website screenshots have been refreshed in English, Spanish and Catalan to show the corrected keyboard.

## Validation and delivery

Nine focused keyboard/practice checks and 24 responsive material states cover both themes, phone/tablet orientations, idle/pressed/guided black keys and retained geometry. Fresh website images use synthetic notebooks and audio tones; no personal data or microphone capture is involved. Final package/signature, installation, website checks and public verification are recorded in adjacent manifests. Native visual acceptance remains separate.

Stable Android 6.0.0/build53, previous beta4 and iOS Simulator beta9/build48 retain their artifacts. Google Play and TestFlight distribution are unchanged.
