# SPino 6.1.0-beta.20 (73)

## Release notes

Audio recording now follows your playing with three expressive animations: Silk waves, Orbit and Mixer. Each new take starts with Silk waves. Swipe one finger in any direction to change styles, or tap the style name. Switching keeps the same recorder, microphone stream and elapsed time.

The waves and orbit respond to microphone intensity; the mixer follows its frequency spectrum. The larger canvas uses colors adapted to light and dark appearance. Motion settles during silence and respects reduced-motion preference. The saved audio remains unchanged by the visual style.

Includes the camera framing, zoom/brightness controls, portrait/landscape format, image rotation and Android recording sharing from beta19. Notebook records, scores, annotations, stable IDs and existing audio/video are retained.
