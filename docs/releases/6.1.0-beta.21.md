# SPino 6.1.0-beta.21 (74)

## Release notes

Complete a planned lesson with its actual date and duration, an optional rating, repertoire scores, comments and a next step. Edit completed lesson updates the same result in history. The original plan and the other appointments in a recurring series stay independent. Completed lessons no longer schedule reminders.

Keep teacher demonstrations with the lesson: associate existing saved audio/video or use Record teacher to capture a new take and link it automatically when saved. Play and Share are available from the lesson and its result. Removing an association keeps the recording; unavailable local files retain their references. Existing scores, annotations, notebook progress and original recording files are preserved.

Includes the Silk waves, Orbit and Mixer audio animations, camera controls and Android sharing from beta20. Lesson totals do not invent a per-score time split; the existing saved-practice editor can record an explicit breakdown.

## Validation and delivery

Build 74 updates the separate Android beta. Responsive browser evidence uses disposable synthetic notebooks and microphone input in light/dark phone/tablet layouts. Focused checks cover lesson completion, stable editing, failed saves/retries, recurring appointments, teacher recording association/capture/playback, backup and cloud-reference preservation. Native replacement installation and data retention are recorded separately. Physical teacher microphone/camera and receiving-app acceptance remain open. Stable Android, Google Play and iOS Simulator downloads stay unchanged.
