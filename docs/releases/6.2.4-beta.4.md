# SPino 6.2.4-beta.4

Android versionCode 132. Owner-requested Android beta for a phone synchronization stall, 26 November 2026. Based on published beta131 (`935c2632`).

## Changes

Stops a stalled Drive adapter operation after three minutes, including its sequence of short requests and local transport-cache waits. Cancels outstanding native requests, blocks late continuations and keeps the serialized operation reserved until it unwinds. Expired operations require an explicit retry instead of starting another automatic retry cycle. Repeated listing/change cursors stop with a specific error before applying partial notebook data. Manual retry reuses retained cache/outbox identities.

## Validation and scope

Synthetic protocol and production-browser tests cover continuous request chains, cyclic pages, blocked cache reads, concurrent cancellation, retained local edits, exact score/annotation content and retry after late responses. Responsive browser evidence is separate from Android installation and physical-device acceptance. The owner's exact phone failure is not reproduced against their real Drive account; successful real-account synchronization remains pending owner installation. This beta fixes verified ways an operation can keep waiting, without claiming that a timeout alone restores connectivity.

Package signatures, web-asset parity, Android unit checks, replacement-install retention and foreground are recorded in the validation manifest. Distribution is the website beta channel; stable 6.2.3 and Play production are retained.
