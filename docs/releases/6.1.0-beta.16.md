# SPino 6.1.0-beta.16 (69)

## Release notes

Record directly from Home or Multimedia without starting a practice session. Use Free recording without a piece, choose one of the horizontal music-stand cards, or use Other pieces to search the repertoire, then choose audio or video. An empty repertoire still permits free recording. The recording mode buttons have equal size and alignment. The recording view shows only capture controls, a live camera preview for video, or a microphone level meter and sound/silence feedback for audio. Video opens a live preview without recording. Select an available device camera before starting, then start, pause, resume or finish the take. Paused time is excluded from the duration. Video frames are normalized locally to portrait (720x1280) or landscape (1280x720) according to device orientation at start, with matching centered preview framing. Rotating the view preserves the current take and its starting format. Stop to review, name and save the take. The suggested title remains until selected, then clears for typing; saving untouched retains the suggestion. Free recordings retain an empty piece association and can copy through the existing notebook-scoped recording sync. Existing recordings, piece IDs and capture from scores are retained.

Practice starts from the saved local notebook without waiting for Drive or requiring Continue locally. Starting practice keeps the existing synchronization setting; incoming changes wait until practice is finished and saved. Multimedia has smaller, uncounted inline audio/video icons, toggleable type filters and a piece filter. Active filters are visible. The header profile-photo circle is slightly larger.



Video includes the original piece title and recording date in a small dark rounded frame with white text and a pale border, positioned inside the lower-left image margin. The date follows the app language. Free takes show only the date. The frame is burned into the saved video and remains visible when shared; playback controls are outside the encoded image. Long titles are shortened visually to fit without changing repertoire or recording metadata.

Independent practices from both devices remain combined during conflict review. The dialog lists retained practices and the exact overlapping values, including a shared plan’s practice link. Selecting Here now retains independent fields in the same record and independently added annotation marks. Catalogue import timestamps no longer create or invalidate sync decisions; older diagnostic-only conflicts can repair automatically. A real change to a reviewed conflicting value still requires a fresh review. Update both Android beta devices before retesting.

## Validation and delivery

Build 69 updates the separate Android beta application. Source, production package, focused browser/unit checks, replacement installation, emulator data retention and public download hashes are recorded in the accompanying validation/publication manifests. Browser synthetic streams and emulator checks remain distinct from physical microphone/camera and two-device Google acceptance. Android stable, Google Play and iOS Simulator downloads are retained.
