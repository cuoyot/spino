# SPino 6.1.1-beta.8 (84) — expanded piano catalogue

## Release notes

The catalogue has 106 conservatively identified works across 129 editions: 55 MusicXML/MXL and 74 PDF. Added 64 complete PDF editions since beta7, including Bach inventions and sinfonias, Burgmüller and Schumann pieces, Satie Gymnopédies, Debussy's second Arabesque and Albéniz's original piano Rumores de la Caleta. Composer and period filters combine with search and downloaded/format views. The work count merges editions and movements; five unresolved legacy identities do not increase the total. Exact edition credits, rights, hashes and bundled first-page previews are retained. Research candidates stay outside the app.

Native select options use explicit theme colors. Catalogue-wide refresh and batch-download controls are removed; individual score downloads remain. Scores, annotations, history and offline Hanon material are retained.
