# SPino 6.1.0-beta.4 (57)

Android beta update authorized for website publication on October 15, 2026.

## Release notes

Double-tap the six-dot grip to minimize the floating practice bar to just the grip and clock. Double-tap again to restore all controls. This works in PDF and MusicXML practice; dragging still moves the bar, and Enter or Space on the focused grip also toggles it.

The expanded bar is 10 px shorter. Narrow phones retain a visible grip and a single row of controls. Minimize and restore preserve practice timing and editing/recording state; the second tap cannot accidentally activate the clock as the controls change size.
