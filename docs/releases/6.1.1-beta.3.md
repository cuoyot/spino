## Release notes

Clearer Hanon 1–10 scores adapt the number of measures to screen width, note duration and notation size. Notes and finger numbers stay inside their measures, with matching clock-cursor alignment in page and scrolling layouts.

Lesson appointments open their linked score directly with practice timing running. Stopping returns to the lesson, and reopening a piece resumes its own accumulated time. Completing the lesson saves measured per-piece times once; additional lesson duration remains unassigned.

Header controls keep their shared row after returning from generated practice, including MIDI, Record, Quick practice and Week/Month. The reader’s column rule is now scoped to its own header.
