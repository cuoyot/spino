# SPino 6.1.0 (76)

## Release notes

Record audio or video for your repertoire, review and share takes, and replay saved recordings offline. Optional Google Drive synchronization keeps the notebook, scores, annotations and saved recording copies available on devices using the same Google account. Unfinished practice stays on its own device; completed history synchronizes. JSON backups include scores and annotations but exclude audio/video files.

Review the last 12 weeks of practice, select weeks and days, and filter detailed time by activity and piece. Saved practice summaries share the same design across Today, Calendar, Progress and piece history. Complete lessons with feedback and recording links. Calendar time controls, repertoire navigation and piece details include the latest reviewed corrections.

Local notebooks, record IDs, scores, annotations and original recordings are preserved. English, Spanish and Catalan, light and dark appearance, MIDI, sight-reading, Hanon, quick practice and the free-practice metronome remain available.

## Distribution

Stable Android application ID: `com.miguecoll.spino`. Signed APK and AAB use version 6.1.0, versionCode 76. The previous beta remains separately installable. Google Play is unchanged until the owner uploads and rolls out the prepared bundle. iOS Simulator remains beta9/build48.

The next beta series is 6.1.1, starting at `6.1.1-beta.1` with a versionCode greater than 76. It is planned, not released.
