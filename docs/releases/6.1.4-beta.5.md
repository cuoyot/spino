# SPino 6.1.4-beta.5 (96) — MIDI first-note recording

MIDI capture waits for the first positive-velocity note. The first note starts at zero; pedal state is captured at that instant. Cancelling or disconnecting while armed creates no empty take. Score controls remain movable and minimizable while waiting. Includes the beta 4 playback scheduling fix.

Website beta delivery; no Play Console submission. Physical keyboard acceptance remains pending.
