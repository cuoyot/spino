# SPino 6.1.4-beta.1 (92) — Practice notifications

## Release notes

Enable notifications in Profile for planned practices and events. Optional practice suggestions encourage a return after more than one calendar day away, progress toward the weekly goal near the weekend, and maintaining a weekly practice streak. At most one suggestion per day at 18:00 local time; planned reminders take priority. Saving an alarm offers to enable notifications if needed.

## Validation and delivery

This beta preserves notebook IDs, scores, annotations and practice history. Suggestions are scheduled seven days ahead and refreshed when opening the app or saving practice. Native packaging, emulator installation, Google Play open-testing submission and website publication evidence are recorded separately. Physical-device acceptance is pending.
