## Changes

Event names and piece titles now sit inside their overview cards. Auditions use a calendar-style date, a weeks/days countdown, integrated Close and separated Edit; the pending-session banner is omitted. Linked pieces and My repertoire show saved attributed practice totals.

Piece details include thumbnail-to-score opening for PDF annotations, saved hours/minutes and the addition date when recorded. Existing entries without a saved date explicitly show Date not recorded; new additions retain their date through edit, restore, backup and cloud merge. Only quiet Close appears above; Practice, Schedule, Edit, Stand and Delete share one Actions menu below the metadata. Existing saved-session cards and notebook/score/annotation identities remain intact.
