# Today

Today shows what is due and gives you one main action: continue an unfinished session, start today's next planned practice, or choose a practice when nothing is planned.

## Start or continue

The first card changes to match your notebook:

- **Resume session** returns to your unfinished session, including one begun on an earlier day. Resume the exercise or timer from its overview when you are ready.
- **Start practice** opens the next planned session's overview. The card shows its context, duration and exercise blocks. **View plan** opens its details, where you can edit, reschedule or delete the appointment.
- **Choose practice** opens Practice, where you can choose an activity or saved routine.

In the current browser build, **Record** sits beside the practice action in this card, including when a practice is planned or unfinished. It opens the standalone recorder. Multimedia also keeps its Record action within the page.

Only one session can be active at a time. Starting another practice while one is unfinished returns you to the existing session. Continue it, save it or discard it before starting another.

## Choose an activity or routine

Open **Practice** in the main navigation whenever you want to choose something to play. Its activity buttons open preparation so you can adjust the session before starting.

- **Sight-reading** and **Technique** support practice without a piano, with a piano, or with a MIDI piano.
- **Repertoire** and **Free practice** require a piano. They start with **With piano** selected.
- **Without piano** uses on-screen input. **With piano** provides manual tracking. **MIDI piano** uses your connected instrument where the exercise supports MIDI evaluation.

**Quick practice** appears with the activity types inside Practice. It opens the untimed, single-hand on-screen exercise directly.

New practices start with a suggested duration of 40 minutes. You can change the duration and exercise settings in preparation.

Your saved routines appear below the activities. Tapping a routine starts it with its saved settings, without opening preparation. For sight-reading and technique, it also opens the exercise when the required input is available. Use **Manage** to edit routines, or find them in **Library**.

## Your week and the rest of today

**Practised this week** highlights your saved practice time from Monday through today. The weekly total and goal show hours and minutes from one hour upward (for example, 199 minutes becomes 3 hr 19 min); shorter durations remain in minutes. If a weekly goal is set in Profile, the card compares those minutes with the goal, shows a progress bar and the minutes remaining, and marks a reached goal. Without a goal, it shows the practised total without a target or progress bar. Saved partial and manually entered practices count; unfinished practice and future-dated records do not. **View progress** opens the detailed Progress page.

The week strip runs from Monday to Sunday. A circle marks today, and colored markers indicate practice activity types and events. Tap a day to open it in the weekly calendar. **View calendar** opens the weekly calendar with today selected.

**Also today** shows the remaining planned practices, events and saved sessions. The practice featured in the first card is not repeated here. A plan and its saved result share one card when they have the same date.

Tap a planned card to see its blocks and options. **Start** opens its session overview; **Edit practice** opens preparation. For a recurring plan, its details also offer cancellation of that appointment and future appointments in the series.

Saved sessions show their **Completed** or **Partial** status, practice time and any rating or notes you entered. Practice logged manually also appears here. Tap a saved session to review its details, read its full notes or edit feedback.

## Events and commitments

Events scheduled for today appear among today's cards. **Coming up** shows the next two future events, with the number of days remaining. Open an event to view its preparation steps and linked repertoire.

Tap **+ Event** on Today to add a lesson, audition, concert or other commitment. Its date starts at today and can be changed before saving. Use Calendar to see additional future events or add an event to a specific day.

In the current browser build, **Repeat** offers every day, every week, every two weeks, every month or every year. **End date (optional)** includes that date; leave it empty for an ongoing series. Monthly and yearly events use the month's last day when the original day does not exist, then return to the original day in later months or years.

Open an occurrence to edit its date, details or preparation steps independently. **Delete event** removes only that occurrence; **Cancel this and future appointments** stops the series from the selected future date (or today for past events). Earlier dates remain. Existing individual events and weekly planner appointments are preserved. Recurring reminders are prepared for the next year when reminders synchronize, subject to the platform's notification capacity; reopening SPino refreshes that window. Native delivery and a package containing this feature remain pending. New recurrence rules require a version that supports them on all synchronized devices.

## Find your music, progress and instrument

**Library** contains repertoire, scores, the catalogue and routines. Tap your avatar to open **Profile**. The **Progress** navigation tab shows the practice-time chart and saved session history; **More details** opens activity/piece filters and date ranges. Set your optional weekly goal in **Profile → Edit profile**. Saved partial and manually logged sessions count toward practice time; unfinished sessions do not.

When synchronization is configured, a small synchronization symbol sits beside MIDI: muted green-grey for a connected state and red for a notebook or recording-copy problem. It has no filled circle or exclamation badge. Tap it to open Backup & sync; conflicts open their review. The accessible label also distinguishes pause, pending changes and transfers.

The small MIDI indicator shows connection state and flashes when input arrives. Tap it to open instrument settings. Connection and input activity are separate from whether the current exercise evaluates MIDI.

The main destinations use a bottom bar on phones and a side rail on larger or short landscape layouts. Rotating the device rearranges the controls while retaining the same notebook.

The first card, **Your week**, combines saved practice time, your optional weekly goal and the seven-day calendar. Colored daily bars show recorded minutes relative to the busiest day that week; select a day to open its calendar. Choose or resume practice immediately below the card.

The unfinished-session card uses the same design on Today and Practice and is also available on other browsing pages. Calendar omits this card to keep the calendar content higher on screen; the Practice navigation indicator remains visible. It shows the session title, elapsed time, context and **Resume session**. A dot on the Practice navigation icon indicates an unfinished session; it disappears when the session is saved or discarded.
