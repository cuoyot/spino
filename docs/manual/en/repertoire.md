# Manage your repertoire

The **My repertoire**, **Catalogue** and **Multimedia** tabs remain visible below the app header as you scroll. Scrolled content stays covered by the navigation band.

Open a piece from **Library → Repertoire** to see its study stage, music-stand membership, musical target, next passage/goal and target tempo. Existing notes and printed tempo remain visible when present.

**Practice** is the main action; **Schedule** prepares a practice for the calendar. The separate score row opens the attached PDF or MusicXML score. The print icon beside it prints that score. Without a local score, the row offers **Add PDF** or the catalogue entry as appropriate.

The **Recordings** row shows the piece's recording count and opens Multimedia. **Record** beside it starts recording for that piece. Select **More options** (the three dots) for **Edit**, **Put on / Take off the stand**, and **Delete work**. Deletion still asks for confirmation and moves the work to Trash, preserving its score, annotations, history and plans. A work opened from Trash offers **Restore work** and its retained session history.

**Sessions for this work** uses the same saved-session cards as Progress, Today and Calendar. Worked passages appear within the shared card. Select the card, or focus it and press Enter, to open the session.

The **Catalogue** combines PDF and MusicXML/MXL scores in one alphabetical list. Search by title or composer, choose **All scores** or **Downloaded**, and optionally filter by format or composer. More results appear as you approach the bottom; **Show more scores** also works with the keyboard. Returning from a score's details keeps your place in the list.

Select the eye icon to inspect a score before saving it. PDF previews show the first page and work offline. MusicXML previews load an opening excerpt using your download network preference. Previews do not add the score to Downloads or your repertoire. Select the download icon to save the complete edition. Once saved, its row offers opening and **Add to repertoire**. Select a title for source/edition details.

**Transfers** combines PDF and MusicXML requests, including progress, retry, pause and completed downloads. The network preference controls transfers; if a browser cannot identify Wi-Fi, allow any connection to download. **View downloaded** returns to your local catalogue.

References without score files, internal analysis reports and editorial research are excluded from the visible catalogue.
