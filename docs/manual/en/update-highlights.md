# Updates and ratings

After updating an existing notebook to SPino 6.2.1, a short dialog summarizes changes to the interface, catalogue and MusicXML viewer. Choose Continue to return to Today, or Rate SPino to open the existing store listing. Rating is optional.

The notice appears once for each release on that installation. It waits while a practice is unfinished or another dialog is open. New installations keep their initial onboarding. This preference stays on the device and does not change your notebook or cloud backup.

MusicXML supports instrument selection, 20–300% zoom and separate visibility controls for instrument names, headings/credits and fingering. Existing score bytes and annotations are retained.
