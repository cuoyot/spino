# Practice rhythm and recordings

In **Progress → Activity**, **Keep your rhythm** shows your consecutive weeks of saved practice, a musical badge and seven dots for practice days in the current week. Select the card to open its monthly calendar. Saved positive practice time marks a day; future sessions and unfinished practice do not count. A single saved practice qualifies a week. The current week remains open until Sunday, so an empty current week does not immediately break the streak earned through last week.

The detail shows the current practice streak, the number of saved sessions within that streak and weekly streak badges beside the monthly calendar. Use the arrows to browse months. The goal streak remains separate and uses your current weekly minutes goal; without a goal it displays a dash. Close the detail with **Close**, Escape or the platform Back action.

The adjacent **Recordings** card counts local audio and video created from Monday through today, including recoverable takes pending their final save. It separates audio and video totals. Select it to open **Library → Multimedia** at the current week.

Use **Record** beside the heading on **Home** or **Multimedia** to record without starting practice. Choose **Free recording** to record without assigning a piece, select a horizontal card from **On your stand**, or open **Other pieces** to search the full repertoire by title/composer. Then choose **Record audio** or **Record video**. Free recording also works with an empty repertoire. Device access is requested only when opening capture. A selected piece filter in Multimedia can preselect that piece.

The recording view shows only the chosen piece, elapsed time and capture controls. Video first opens the live camera preview without recording. Choose an available **Camera** (when the device provides more than one), then **Start recording**. **Pause recording** freezes the recording clock; **Resume recording** continues the same take. The preview is muted locally to avoid feedback. The video format follows portrait or landscape orientation when recording starts. The preview and output use the same centered framing. The frame is normalized locally to 720x1280 portrait or 1280x720 landscape; rotating the view during recording retains the take’s original format and recorder. Audio shows the measured microphone level, **Listening…** during silence and **Sound detected** while receiving a signal. The piece stays fixed during that take. Select **Stop and review**, then keep/edit the title and **Save**, or **Discard**. The title suggestion remains visible until you select its field; selection clears it and opens typing. Saving without selecting it retains the suggestion. A standalone take links to the chosen piece, or has no piece when recorded freely, and does not create a practice session. Free takes show no View piece action. Leaving the app stops capture and retains the pending review; Escape or Back does not discard an active or unsaved take.

In **Multimedia**, the calendar opens with one week. Select the month/year heading (for example, **October 2026**) to expand the full month; select it again to return to the week. The heading contains only month and year, naming both months (and both years when needed) for a week crossing the boundary. There is no repeated day range or chevron. There are no separate Week/Month buttons. The arrows move backward or forward by one week or month according to the displayed view. The recording list automatically follows that period. Use **Audio** or **Video**, or a matching icon in a calendar day, to filter the displayed period by recording type. Select the same type again to clear it; choosing the other switches type. Active filters show a checkmark and border, with pressed states for assistive technology. **Piece** opens the list of recorded pieces. Choose one to combine it with the type and period, or **All pieces** to clear the piece filter. Its title stays visible on the button. The weekly Progress shortcut clears existing filters. Opening Multimedia from a piece starts at the week containing its most recent local recording, while showing all recordings in that period.

Only dates containing recordings matching the active filters receive a colored background; empty dates remain neutral, including today. Audio is teal and video coral. Mixed dates combine both colors. Each populated day shows microphone/audio and camera/video icons side by side without visible counts. Accessible date and icon labels retain exact totals. Select either icon to toggle its type filter across the displayed period. If the period is empty, the list says that there are no recordings this week or month. With filters active, it reports that no recordings match those filters.

Recording-list icons use the same audio/video colors. Existing playback, review/save, title editing and deletion controls remain available.

In **Progress → Activity → History**, each saved practice shows its date, duration, practice types and manual-entry or instrument context. Pieces keep their original titles. A locally available PDF or MXL score appears as a thumbnail; a missing or unreadable score keeps the practice icon. Existing feedback appears as a large emoji with its rating label. A practice without feedback shows no rating. Select anywhere on the card, or focus it and press Enter, to open the saved session; **View session** is a quiet cue within that same action. Partial practice remains labeled.
