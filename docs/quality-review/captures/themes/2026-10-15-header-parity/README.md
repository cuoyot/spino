# Equal primary headers — October 15, 2026

Owner reports that Calendar and Library change header height relative to Progress and the other main routes. Calendar's padded selector enlarged its grid row; Library's tabs stacked inside the primary header on phones. The primary header now has a fixed 68 CSS px height, with enough internal room for the existing 54 px selector. Title, Profile and MIDI share identical positions across routes at each viewport size. Calendar keeps Week/Month beside its title with complete labels and 44 px choice targets. Narrow-screen title sizing and horizontal spacing are shared across all main routes.

Library's My repertoire/Catalogue tabs now occupy their own sticky row at the top of the page, below the primary header. They retain their flat tab treatment, active state, 48 px targets and reachability when the page scrolls. Long detail titles use at most two visual lines; the full original text remains in the notebook, heading DOM and title tooltip. No record, PDF or annotation changes.
