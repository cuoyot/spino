## Concrete acquisition leads

- **43, Albéniz Tango:** https://urresearch.rochester.edu/institutionalPublicationPublicView.action?institutionalItemId=19692&versionNumber=1 . Sibley holds a historical piano edition. Existing albeniz-priority.md records the exact binary and Hugo Ries editorial-rights hold; that hold remains. Search an unedited historical edition in parallel.
- **28, Schubert D.899 No.2:** https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1551 . Piano; Breitkopf & Härtel 1888; typesetter public-domain dedication; PDF link on item. Prior exact-file checks in romantic-file-checks.json.
- **36, Schumann Träumerei:** https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=504 . Piano; Leichte Stucke 1900; public-domain dedication. Historical source/editor unresolved; review before import.
- **32, Chopin Revolutionary Étude:** https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/ChopinFF/O10/op-10-12-wfi/op-10-12-wfi-a4.pdf . Mutopia lists Peters/Herrmann Scholtz, 1900b and Public Domain. Requires complete binary/page inspection and attribution manifest before import.
- **40, Lange Blumenlied:** https://scholarexchange.furman.edu/periphery-russian1/17/ . University-held 1866 score; institutional statement clears US only and expressly does not determine other countries. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blumenlied,_Op.39_(IA_imslp-op39-lange-gustav).pdf is a second historical-scan lead. Check editor/edition/territories.
- **49, Satie Je te veux:** https://imslp.org/wiki/Je_te_veux_(Satie,_Erik_Alfred_Leslie) . Distinguish original voice-and-piano from solo-piano versions and modern arrangements.

## Next acquisition order

Albéniz Tango first; then Arietta, Träumerei, Frühlingslied, Blumenlied and Schubert D.899 No.2 for useful short piano repertoire. Follow with Chopin Op.64 No.1, Op.66, Op.10 Nos.3/12, Debussy Préludes I/8 and Children’s Corner No.6, and Satie’s own piano version of Je te veux. Duets and orchestral reductions require independent version checks.

The book’s stars rate its own versions, including simplified arrangements, and must not be copied as ratings for original scores. The book identifies BWV Anh.114 as Bach; retain Petzold attribution in the app. All named composers died by 1934, making historical originals promising public-domain candidates in Spain; this is not clearance of the photographed modern publication, particular arrangements, editors, lyrics, or every distribution territory.
