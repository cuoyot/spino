## What rights are actually identified?

### Takeaway
The exact modern editions have an explicit public-domain dedication, CC BY 3.0, or CC BY-SA 4.0 declaration. A composer's age alone does not establish the status of editorial additions or scan rights; preserve the individual declaration and source history.

### Cited Findings
- Mutopia explicitly allows redistribution and commercial use of its CC BY and BY-SA material with attribution; BY-SA adaptations must use the same license. Its public-domain label states that the contributor dedicated their contribution to the public domain. This addresses the new typesetting contribution, while historical underlying material still needs examination. [Mutopia licensing policy](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html).
- CC BY 3.0 candidates: Chopin Op. 6 No. 1, Schumann Op. 15 No. 2, Liszt S. 172 No. 3. Their exact edition declarations and contributor identities are cited in each table row. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- CC BY-SA 4.0 candidates: Chopin Op. 9 No. 3 and Grieg Op. 12 No. 7. Keep attribution and license with previews and any score transformations; the table points to each exact licensed edition. [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Spain has a special transitional copyright-duration provision for authors who died before 7 December 1987; do not use a generic EU term as the sole clearance test. [Spanish Copyright Act, transitional provision four](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930#dtcuarta).
- The U.S. Copyright Office currently states that works published in the United States before 1 January 1931 have expired copyright. This is a U.S. rule, not worldwide clearance for every edition. [U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/).

### Inferences
- A licensed modern re-engraving with a specific nineteenth-century source is preferable to an unidentified downloaded scan. Do not strip the PDF credit line or assume that a public-domain composition allows a modern publisher's edition to be copied.
- Keep a rights record per asset: original composer; exact work identity; historic edition publisher/year/editor; modern typesetter; license URL/version; source URL; PDF hash; review date; territorial decision. No proprietary difficulty metadata is needed for this batch.

### Gaps
- Final worldwide territorial clearance was not established. Some pages omit the historical editor; Brahms Op. 39 No. 15 also lacks a source publication year. Retrieve the historic title/copyright pages before treating those fields as verified.
- Do not assert that an anonymous contributor's dedication resolves undisclosed third-party editorial rights.

## What source inconsistencies and next steps matter?

### Takeaway
The backlog is actionable at the file level, but needs canonical-identity and edition review before adding records. Several inherited metadata labels should be corrected or left unknown rather than copied into the app.

### Cited Findings
- Both Schumann pages give the impossible composition date “18th C.” for a composer whose page dates are 1810–1856. Keep the work number; do not import that date. [Op. 15 No. 2](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=355); [Op. 15 No. 7](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=504).
- Grieg's Albumblatt is identified as Op. 12 No. 7 on the source page, while its download directory is named `No03_Albumblatt`. Treat the path as an asset name, not a canonical work number; visually verify the title against the historic source. [Exact edition](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2194).
- Grieg Troldtog's contributor discloses changes to some crescendo and diminuendo markings. Preserve that note in provenance rather than claiming a diplomatic reproduction. [Exact edition](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2014).
- Old French Song's source page labels the style “Classical”; this research groups it under Tchaikovsky/Romantic rather than blindly adopting the source's style field. [Exact edition](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2080).
