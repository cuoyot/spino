# Additional periods and traditions for piano and keyboard repertoire

## Which additional periods and traditions are useful?

### Takeaway
Extend the research to Renaissance keyboard music, the galant/preclassical transition, national traditions within Romanticism, ragtime, early twentieth-century repertoire, and contemporary music explicitly released under open licences. Treat these as research candidates, not newly included or territorially cleared catalogue entries.

### Cited Findings
- Renaissance keyboard: William Byrd, **The Carman's Whistle**, T 444 / FVB 58. IMSLP identifies keyboard/harpsichord instrumentation and offers the 1789 Charles Burney edition, complete score #487690, 3 pages, marked Public Domain. Prefer this early edition for review over assuming every modern edition is reusable. — [Score and exact edition](https://imslp.org/wiki/The_Carman%27s_Whistle,_T_444_(Byrd,_William))
- Additional Renaissance lead: Gibbons's **The Lord of Salisbury his pavin** is listed in *Early Keyboard Music*, a collection explicitly for virginal, spinet, harpsichord and clavichord. This is a title/source lead only; the precise edition rights require further checking. — [Collection contents](https://imslp.org/wiki/Early_Keyboard_Music_(Oesterle,_Louis))
- Galant/preclassical transition: C. P. E. Bach, **Rondo in E-flat Major**, H.288, for harpsichord/piano. The Mutopia edition derives from J. Maho, 1870s, is marked Public Domain and provides a PDF. Mutopia itself calls the style Classical; the transition label is a catalogue/research interpretation. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=177)
- Spanish national tradition: Isaac Albéniz, **Rumores de la Caleta**, Op.71, piano, 1887. Mutopia records Unión Musical Española as source, Public Domain as copyright status, and PDF downloads. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=898)
- Norwegian national tradition: Grieg, **Albumblatt in E minor**, Op.12 No.7, piano, 1867. Mutopia's edition is based on G. Schirmer 1902 and licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**; it is not a public-domain engraving. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2194)
- Grieg, **Troldtog / March of the Dwarfs**, Op.54 No.3, piano. Mutopia identifies C. F. Peters circa 1891 as source, Public Domain status and PDF availability. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=2014)
- Ragtime: Scott Joplin, **Peacherine Rag** (1901). Mutopia identifies a reproduction of the original 1901 edition, Public Domain status and a piano PDF. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=335)
- Joplin, **Bethena** (1905), piano; Mutopia identifies an original-manuscript source, Public Domain status and a PDF. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=463)
- Joplin, **Solace** (1909), piano; Mutopia identifies an original-manuscript source, Public Domain status and a PDF. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=482)
- Early twentieth-century bridge: Scriabin, **Prelude**, Op.59 No.2 (1910), piano. Mutopia identifies a Dover reproduction source and Public Domain edition with PDF; its style tag is Romantic, so a chronology filter should coexist with style rather than overwrite it. — [Exact score](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=445)
- Contemporary original composition: Grigor Iliev, **A Child's Wish** (2006), piano. The composer is the listed source/maintainer and the edition is **CC BY 3.0**, with PDF and LilyPond downloads. — [Exact score and licence](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=727)
- Contemporary original composition: Stéphane Magnenat, **Variations d'automne** (2007), piano, source “Own work”, **CC BY-SA 3.0**, with PDF and LilyPond downloads. — [Exact score and licence](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=973)

### Inferences
- The ten fully described score-page candidates above (excluding the provisional Gibbons lead) offer concrete diversity without repeating the already known Joplin *The Entertainer* / *Maple Leaf Rag* or Satie/Debussy selections.
- Nationalism and ragtime are useful parallel traditions/style filters, not mutually exclusive chronological periods. Renaissance candidates should be labelled keyboard repertoire playable on piano; they were not originally composed for the modern piano.
- Prioritize Iliev as an especially clear contemporary rights lead: the composer supplies the score under an explicit permissive licence, rather than relying on age alone.

### Gaps
- No binary files were downloaded, hashed, rendered or included in the app in this research pass. Existing XML/PDF catalogue duplicates must still be checked by canonical work identity.
- A repository's Public Domain label is an edition-level lead, not a claim that every jurisdiction and every underlying editorial contribution has been cleared.
- Gibbons and additional Sweelinck works need a stronger exact-edition review. In particular, *Ballo del Granduca*, SwWV 319, has a disputed attribution on the repository page, so do not present it as unqualified Sweelinck authorship. — [Authorship note](https://imslp.org/wiki/Ballo_del_Granduca,_SwWV_319_(Sweelinck,_Jan_Pieterszoon))

## How can contemporary and historic editions be reused responsibly?

### Takeaway
Keep composition rights, edition rights, source provenance and licence obligations separate. Explicitly open contemporary music is viable alongside historical public-domain music.

### Cited Findings
- CC BY 3.0 allows redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use, with credit, licence linkage and the applicable modification notices. It forbids additional legal/technical restrictions on the licensed freedoms. — [Creative Commons licence deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
- CC BY-SA 3.0 also permits commercial sharing/adaptation, but adaptations must retain the same or a compatible licence, plus attribution and notices. — [Creative Commons licence deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Mutopia distinguishes Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA and older licences; individual piece pages must be checked rather than treating the entire repository as uniformly licensed. — [Mutopia licensing](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html)

### Inferences
- Prefer CC0/Public Domain/CC BY for the simplest operational path; BY-SA can remain eligible if the score distribution and any adapted score satisfy its terms. This does not require claiming the entire application becomes BY-SA.
- Do not promote NC-licensed arrangements into the unrestricted app merely because the underlying composer is ancient.

### Gaps
- This pass does not determine worldwide public-domain status or provide jurisdiction-specific legal conclusions. Territorial and edition checks remain part of each candidate's inclusion review.
