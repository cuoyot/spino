## Which distinct works can form an actionable first acquisition batch?

### Takeaway

The table identifies 15 distinct work candidates across eight composers, plus Couperin as a collection requiring piece-level extraction. Group all movements of a sonata or suite under one work identity when counting distinct works; treat alternative editions as assets of that work. Avoid the previously supplied existing-work exclusions, and perform a final local catalogue identity match before acquisition.

## Which further composers and institutional collections should be searched next?

### Takeaway

Couperin has a directly available licensed keyboard collection. Handel requires exact HWV identification for generic titles. Rameau and a historical Mozart institutional collection are useful leads, but should remain research entries until exact work and file/rights checks are complete.

### Cited Findings

- François Couperin, *L'Art de toucher le clavecin* (1716), harpsichord, based on Foucaut 1716; Nicolas Sceaux's 2007 typesetting is CC BY 2.5. [Record](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=907), [29-page PDF checked](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/CouperinF/ArtDeToucherLeClavecin/ArtDeToucherLeClavecin-a4.pdf). Treat as a collection; map the individual preludes/allemande from the score before deciding work-level counts. Not counted in the 15 above.
- Handel's Mutopia keyboard listing includes *Impertinence*, *Gavotte*, *Entrée*, etc., based on Edition Schott 1930 and labelled PD. [Listing](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/make-table.cgi?Composer=HandelGF), [Impertinence record](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=154). The generic catalogue titles are insufficient here to assign a confidently verified HWV identity. They remain identification candidates, not approved works.
- Bibliothek der Musik-Akademie Basel supplies Mozart's *Trois Sonates pour le Clavecin ou le Forte Piano*, Mannheim: Götz, not before 1783, 30 pages, shelfmark MAB Q19; item marked Public Domain Mark. [Institutional record](https://www.e-rara.ch/mab/content/titleinfo/23135412), [persistent DOI](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-81696). Item-level terms request citation, information and a copy/address of reuse. This is a useful alternate historical Mozart source, but the Köchel identities and actual score binary were not checked.
- Rameau's 1724 *Pièces de clavecin avec une méthode pour la mécanique des doigts* is identified by the Rameau project catalogue. [Project record](https://temp.rameau2014.fr/index.php/fre/RESSOURCES/Catalogue/Musique-instrumentale/PIECES-DE-CLAVECIN-AVEC-UNE-METHODE-POUR-LA-MECANIQUE-DES-DOIGTS). Search indexing exposed the first-edition description, but opening failed with 502. No institutional score binary with item-level commercial reuse terms was verified in this pass; do not acquire a modern *Tambourin* engraving merely because the composition is old.

### Inferences

- The institutional Mozart item may resolve the desire for earlier sources, but only if its contents match a needed non-duplicate work. Its three-sonata collection must never be counted as three unspecified new works without inspecting contents.
- Couperin, Handel and Rameau fill French/English Baroque coverage missing from a Bach-heavy catalogue; exact edition acquisition remains the next step.

### Gaps

- Exact HWV numbers for Handel's generic Mutopia titles, Rameau item-level reusable files, and Mozart e-rara Köchel numbers remain unresolved.
- This short pass concentrated on directly actionable editions; it is not an exhaustive holdings survey of national libraries.

## What rights and counting rules should gate inclusion?

### Takeaway

Edition-specific PD/CC declarations are actionable evidence; they are not substitutes for exact-asset provenance, attribution and territory review. Publication in an app should follow a rights ledger per asset and a distinct identity per musical work.

### Cited Findings

- Mutopia's own legal page states that the PD label covers the contributor's dedication, and that its CC BY and CC BY-SA music may be reused commercially with credit; adaptations of BY-SA material retain the specified licence. It also disclaims warranties. [Mutopia legal terms](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [CC BY 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/).
- Mutopia's BWV814 record explicitly requires section-specific contributor attribution, an example of why one collection-level maintainer credit is insufficient. [Record](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=100).

### Inferences

- For each acquisition, retain source record URL, exact original PDF URL, fetch date, bytes/hash, source edition/publisher/editor/year where known, contributor, exact licence/version and public attribution. Preserve score footer credits and mark any extraction/crop/retypesetting.
- Do not claim worldwide legal clearance from composer death dates alone. Spain/EU and the app's distribution territories need composition, edition/editor and scan/source terms considered independently. Mutopia's PD statement is evidence, not a legal guarantee.
- Count BWV848 once, each complete sonata once, each alternate edition once as an asset rather than a new work. Never add unresolved research placeholders to the visible catalogue.

### Gaps

- No legal opinion or exhaustive jurisdiction analysis was performed. Final inclusion review remains required, especially for the Mozart 1951 edition and source editions whose editor is unspecified.
