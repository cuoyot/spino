# European public and national library sources for piano scores

Research date: 2026-10-21. This is a research shortlist, not an ingestion manifest. No source or edition below has been added to SPino. A source's Public Domain Mark is evidence of its stated assessment, not independent clearance in every distribution territory. Composition, edition/arrangement, and reproduction must be tracked separately.

## Which repositories are useful and what do their rights and interfaces permit?

### Takeaway
Prioritize BNE's specifically cleared public-domain reproductions, Swiss e-rara records bearing Public Domain Mark, and MDZ records explicitly bearing Public Domain Mark. Gallica, Austrian Google scans, and modern Danish scholarly editions have material reuse restrictions despite free downloads.

### Inferences
- The strongest expansion route is a small, reviewed selection of PDM scans from e-rara/MDZ and BNE's explicitly reusable public-domain images, rather than mass importing national-library search results.
- A first-page thumbnail/preview is a more defensible discovery aid than inventing a numerical difficulty from titles. Difficulty should remain unknown until evaluated against the exact score; catalogue genre or “piano” is not a rating.
- For every candidate save composition author/death data, edition/editor/arranger/imprint, reproduction rights URI, attribution text, catalogue persistent ID, download URL, checksum and review date separately.

### Gaps
- Item-level rights clearance for worldwide distribution, beyond the source's statement, remains open.
- No institutional standardized piano difficulty/rating system was established in these repositories.
- MusicXML was not established for these historical-library sources; expect scanned PDFs/images.

## Which exact editions are promising, with downloadable-file evidence?

### Takeaway
Three exact e-rara editions have institution-published download entries and explicit PDM statements. They are candidates for a later acquisition/visual QA pass, **not verified binary downloads in this session**: the web PDF fetch was inaccessible and direct Python network access failed DNS. Metadata and advertised file sizes are actual access evidence; successful `%PDF`/checksum validation remains required.

### Cited Findings
1. **Ludwig van Beethoven — Grande Sonate pour le Pianoforte, op. 53 (Waldstein)**. Vienna/Pest, J. Riedl, **after 1815**; Musik-Akademie Basel **MAB O 2635**. [Stable DOI](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-144348); [item and explicit PDM/download entry, 13.76 MB](https://www.e-rara.ch/mab/content/titleinfo/31352611). [Advertised-pattern PDF](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/31352611.pdf); [actual linked IIIF manifest](https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/31352611/manifest). Source credits composer, institution, shelfmark and DOI. Piano solo; no rated difficulty supplied. This is an advanced-repertoire candidate by musical judgement, not a source rating.
2. **Ernst Friedrich Hegar (arranger) — Erheiterungen für Pianofortespieler / Recréations pour les pianistes**. Basel, F. Hegar, **[1860?]**, **two-hand piano**, 2 volumes/10 booklets bound together; Musik-Akademie Basel **MAB O 902**. [Stable DOI](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-79522); [item, explicit PDM and 32.98 MB download entry](https://www.e-rara.ch/mab/content/titleinfo/22372202). [PDF mechanism](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/22372202.pdf); [linked IIIF manifest](https://www.e-rara.ch/i3f/v20/22372202/manifest). Mixed waltzes, marches, arias and potpourris: requires per-piece extraction/identification and exact underlying composer verification. No standardized difficulty.
3. **Daniel Steibelt — Trois Sonates Pour Le Forte Piano, op. 37, avec Accompagnement de Violon ad Libitum**. Paris, Pleyel, **[18--?]**; Universitätsbibliothek Basel **UBH kk XVIII 1213:2 + 1215:6**. [Stable DOI](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-102590); [item with explicit PDM, 18.72 MB download and separate violin/keyboard PDFs](https://www.e-rara.ch/bau_1/content/titleinfo/28261593). [Whole-object PDF mechanism](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/28261593.pdf). The title explicitly calls violin accompaniment optional; inspect keyboard part before piano-solo inclusion. No standardized difficulty.
4. **Alexander Scriabin — Sonate-fantaisie no. 2, op. 19**. Leipzig, Belaïeff, **1898**, 19 pages. [MDZ exact edition](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb11897112). The viewer references [this IIIF manifest](https://api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v2/bsb11897112/manifest), but fetch failed and item rights were not visible: **discovery-only, do not ingest**. Composer identification should be confirmed from manifest/title page.

### Inferences
- Hegar may offer more immediately useful short browsing selections than a complete large sonata, but every component must be identified and visually checked.
- Preserve collections as collections until their internal structure is verified; do not expose a multi-piece PDF as many apparent downloadable works without correct page ranges/files.

### Gaps
- No binary PDF successfully fetched in this environment; do not claim download validation.
- No 3–5 newly reusable BNE/BC exact editions were established within this bounded pass. The Swiss items are the strongest concrete candidates; BNE is the next targeted discovery task.
- Page count, completeness, scan legibility on phones and title-page edition/arranger checks remain outstanding for all candidates.
