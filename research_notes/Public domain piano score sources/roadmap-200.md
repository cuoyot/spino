# Acquisition roadmap toward 200 distinct playable pieces

Research date 2026-10-21. Planning only: no catalogue entries or assets changed. Current baseline supplied by coordinator: 65 editions, including 6 Burgmüller op.100 pieces and 1 Bach Invention; further MusicXML overlaps and unique-work count not yet audited. Edition count is not unique-work count.

## What counts are verified?

### Takeaway
The proposed five-collection core totals **207 numbered pieces/studies**, but **100 are Czerny exercises**. A better repertoire-first candidate pool replaces Czerny with Grieg and Clementi: **179 musical pieces/studies/sonatinas**, from which we can select genuinely new material after deduplication. Neither total is a ready-to-publish catalogue count.

### Inferences
- Five-collection arithmetic: 100 + 25 + 43 + 24 + 15 = **207**, comprising 100 technical exercises and 107 other pieces/studies. This is not a recommendation to use exercises to fill the target.
- Balanced candidate pool: 25 + 43 + 24 + 15 + 66 + 6 = **179**. Known seven overlaps reduce potential additions to at most **172**, before other duplicates and suitability/rights rejections. This is a pipeline with a reserve margin, not a promise of 172 cleared scores.
- If the existing 65 editions really represent 65 distinct qualifying pieces, we need 135 additions. The 179 pool can supply 135 if total overlaps/rejections are at most 44. If baseline unique count is lower, the shortfall rises accordingly.
- Practical batches: complete missing Burgmüller + Bach first (up to33); Schumann + Tchaikovsky next (up to67); then select from Grieg/Clementi (up to72). With only the known7 overlaps this reaches 65 + 33 + 67 =165, then needs35 additional distinct pieces from the last72. More overlap means selecting further reserve pieces.
- Keep studies visibly identified as studies; keep the 100 Czerny drills in an optional exercises category rather than using them as the central acquisition claim.

### Gaps
- Full audit by composer + catalogue/opus + number + instrumentation is required. Do not dedupe solely by translated title or download filename.
- No verified reusable full PDF for Grieg66, Clementi6 or Czerny100 was acquired in this brief follow-up. Publisher count sources are evidence of counts only, not permission to copy modern editions.

## Which complete downloadable editions were located, and what remains?

### Takeaway
Three complete historical PDF editions were located as repository-listed candidates. One has a direct Sibley institutional record; two have source-file entries in IMSLP. Availability and repository rights statements are established at metadata level; binary download, page completeness, legal review by distribution territory and phone legibility remain pending. No PDF was ingested.

### Cited Findings
- **Burgmüller op.100 complete**: Schott [1852], plate11509, reprinted G.Schirmer, undated. [Sibley institutional record, handle1802/14955](https://hdl.handle.net/1802/14955). [Repository mirror listing](https://imslp.org/wiki/25_%C3%89tudes_faciles_et_progressives,_Op.100_(Burgm%C3%BCller,_Friedrich)) advertises complete PDF **#213101**, 8.45MB,37pages, scanned US-R, Public Domain. An alternative Schirmer1903/Oesterle complete score is #337046,6.46MB,36pages; Oesterle died1932. Prefer Sibley's institutional record and exact source-file/licence before acquisition. Avoid assuming Gallica's separate scans share unrestricted commercial reuse.
- **Schumann op.68 complete**: Schuberth1849 second edition, plate1232, **#300406**, 3.71MB,68pages, Public Domain. [Repository catalogue and download entry](https://imslp.org/wiki/Album_f%C3%BCr_die_Jugend,_Op.68_(Schumann,_Robert)); [actual download resolver](https://imslp.org/wiki/Special:ImagefromIndex/300406). The resolver returned a friendly-redirect page in this environment, so direct binary retrieval is pending. The same catalogue also lists Clara Schumann's1887 edition, #51556,9MB,50pages, marked Public Domain; do not count it as another43 works.
- **Tchaikovsky op.39 complete**: Schirmer1904, editor Adolf Ruthardt(1849–1934), plate17224, **#07996**, 2.81MB,31pages, marked Public Domain. [Repository catalogue with exact complete score entry](https://imslp.org/wiki/Children's_Album,_Op.39_(Tchaikovsky,_Pyotr)). This is distinct from its modern reprint sold by Hal Leonard and from later edited Russian editions. The full-file source entry is verified; binary validation and jurisdiction assessment remain pending.
- **Useful individual-file fallback**: Mutopia's actual [Burgmüller op.100 no.15 PDF](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BurgmullerJFF/O100/25EF-15/25EF-15-a4.pdf) is accessible through the web parser and visibly states Public Domain. This proves an actual usable individual score exists but does not establish that all25 Mutopia numbers are present or equally licensed. [Mutopia's project listing](https://ibiblio.net/mutopia/piece-list.html) identifies other op.100 pieces.

### Inferences
- Acquisition gate per numbered piece: exact edition and rights evidence; successful PDF/XML download and checksum; complete independently accessible piece (or correct source collection/page mapping); composer/opus dedupe; preview render; manual musical suitability review.
- A full anthology should remain downloadable as an anthology or supply correctly extracted component files; assigning179 names to six full-book downloads without useful navigation is not a finished catalogue.

### Gaps
- The notes deliberately do not label repository metadata as a completed download audit.
- Ratings from commercial publishers can inform human research, but do not mass copy proprietary commentary or invent ratings for uninspected historical editions.
